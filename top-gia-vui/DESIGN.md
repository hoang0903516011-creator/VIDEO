## What NOT to Do
- No invented product claims, certifications, reviews or crossed-out prices.
- No invented promotions: use only 6 gói • 150.000đ, 20–31/10, TikTok Đồ Gia dụng tốt.
- Do not cover the character's face or six-package display with offer text.
- Keep important text out of rightmost 140px and bottom 240px.
- Do not claim AI photo animation is live-action footage or lip-synced avatar video.
